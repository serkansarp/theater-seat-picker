EN: A theater seat selection app. Pick seats on an interactive seating chart, with different prices for front and back rows and a live summary of your selected seats and total cost. Selections are limited to 8 seats, and pressing "Satın al" saves them in the browser so they are restored on your next visit. Built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with keyboard-friendly buttons and reduced-motion support.

Developed with the assistance of AI (Claude), under my own direction and review.
